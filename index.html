FAQs

Questions answered

What do I need to send to get started?+

Your logo, brand colors, the products you want to feature, and the campaign or promo you are running. Product photos help too, but I can work from what you have.

Which platforms are the frames made for?+

Frames are designed in vertical 9:16 for phone live streams such as TikTok Live and Shopee Live, with space left for each platform’s comments and buttons.

Can the frame be animated?+

Yes. Frames can be static or include motion, like moving backgrounds, floating products or looping price highlights, as in the VIVA Payday frame.

How long does a frame take?+

It depends on the scope and whether it is animated. Tell me your live date and I will confirm a timeline before we start.
