About me

I’m Zakaria Wirawanto, a graphic designer specializing in live streaming frame design. I create visuals that balance the host, products, and promotional messages, helping your brand make the most of every screen.

A live frame works for hours on a small phone screen while the host moves and talks. I design for that: clear space for the host, products where viewers look first, and prices that stay readable from the first minute to the last.

What goes into one frame

Four zones, planned before any decoration, so the frame still works when the host leans in or holds a product to the camera.

Headline zoneCampaign name and logo, top of screen.
Host zoneKept clear in the middle third.
Offer zonePrice, bundle and bonus cards.
Platform-safe areaSpace left for comments and buttons.
